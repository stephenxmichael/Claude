T.10  /  Why People Stop Watching
They Will Leave
On The Audio.
You can have great lighting, sharp visuals and a clean setup. If your audio is bad, people leave almost instantly.
T.11  —  Built-In
Your camera or phone’s built-in microphone can work in quiet environments, but it often picks up echo, distance and background noise. If your voice sounds far away or unclear, your message loses impact quickly, even if your visuals look great.
T.11  —  External
A lav or external microphone brings your voice closer, reduces background noise, and instantly improves clarity. Any wireless mic will give better audio than straight from a phone or camera.
