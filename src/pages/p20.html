T.2  /  Why ISO Matters
Brightness
Has A Price
Higher ISO → brighter image
Lower ISO → darker image
Sensitivity to light
ISO controls the brightness of your image by adjusting how sensitive your camera is to light.
How To Use It
Start with the lowest ISO for your environment.
→
Bright environments keep ISO low
→
Indoor or low light increase ISO gradually
If your image is too dark, you can raise ISO. But first consider opening your aperture or slowing your shutter speed to maintain better image quality.
