04Your signature series

Rediscovering her

Not “watch me recover.” It’s “I’m rediscovering myself in a new chapter, and maybe you are too.”

It’s a creative thread, not a schedule. Let it shape your Monday talks and Wednesday vlogs when an idea fits; not every post needs the label.

Ideas to draw from
  • What do I even like?
  • Who was I before all the roles?
  • Making Houston feel like home
  • Take yourself anyway
  • Getting dressed for me again
  • Making friends as a grown woman
  • Learning to enjoy my own company
  • Things I’m bringing into my next chapter
An optional opener, when you’re ready

“I knew who I was as a wife, but after my divorce, I realized I had to rediscover who I was as a woman.”

Maybe you’re rediscovering her too.

08Women’s safety education

Approachable. Responsible.

Your Houston instruction business is still taking shape, so this month is about introduction, not promotion: help women see you as someone they can learn from.

This month · Talk to the camera

Your story, your perspective, and the questions beginners have are enough. Classes and classroom footage can become content once your business is up and running.

Topics to grow into
  • Your instructor story, stated accurately
  • Common concerns beginners have
  • Misconceptions about women and firearm education
  • Responsible ownership, at a high level
  • Why safe storage and ongoing education matter
Keep it responsible
  • Focus on confidence and education, not tactics or demonstrations.
  • Plan and verify any future care or cleaning demo yourself, using the manufacturer’s guidance.
  • Check each platform’s current rules before posting.
Invite interest, without taking bookings

“What would you want to learn first?”  ·  “Would you like updates when I start offering classes in Houston?”

09Filming and editing

Keep it focused. Keep it you.

Camera talks · Mon & safety Fri

  1. Begin with your main point.

    Your first sentence is the reason to keep watching.

  2. One idea per video.

    Save a second idea for another week.

  3. Sound like yourself.

    Editing should tighten you, not change you.

  4. Test 30–60 seconds.

    Go longer when the story needs it.

Mini vlogs · The Pilates example
  1. 1

    Beginning

    “I almost talked myself out of Pilates today.”

  2. 2

    Experience

    “But I went anyway.” Your outfit, a detail, you in class (with the studio’s permission).

  3. 3

    Reflection

    “Here’s why I’m glad I came.” That’s your ending.

Shot recipe: a wide view, a detail, and you taking part.

11Trust before monetization

Recommendations start with trust

People buy what you recommend when they already believe you. That belief comes from watching you genuinely use what you share.

  1. 1

    Show what you use

    Clothing, accessories, and everyday products that are really part of your life.

  2. 2

    Let it appear naturally

    In your outfits, routines, and vlogs, without a sales pitch.

  3. 3

    Notice the questions

    “Where’s that from?” is a signal. Keep a list of what people ask about.

  4. 4

    Recommend, when it fits

    Later, through TikTok Shop or other ways, for items your audience already trusts you on.

Your first milestone

Then apply for TikTok Shop creator access once your account is eligible. TikTok sets the requirements, so we’ll confirm them first. It’s a goal, not a guarantee of approval or income; replacing your income comes from steady revenue over time, not a follower count.

12Your first-month review

Look back first

At the end of the month, open your TikTok analytics, note your follower counts, and tick each question once you’ve answered it.

Starting TikTok followers
Ending TikTok followers

  • Which posts brought in new followers?

  • Which got meaningful comments or shares?

  • Which got outfit, accessory, or product questions?

    These point toward future recommendations.

  • Which got interest in safety education?

  • Did three posts a week feel sustainable?

    What you can keep doing matters as much as what performs.

Then repeat what worked. Your answers shape Months 2 and 3, including which Rediscovering Her ideas to film next.
