Tier 03
Full Creator Rig
Built to shoot all day without thinking about the gear once.
Body
Cage + side handle
Mounting points for mic, light and monitor. Runs cooler.
Motion
Gimbal
Movement that holds. Slower and smoother than your hands.
Storage
External SSD
Record straight out of Blackmagic. No transfer afterwards.
Light
Second light or bounce
Separates you from the background, or fills the shadow side.
Buy In Order
Finish a tier before you start the next one. A gimbal will not fix bad audio, and a cage will not fix a shot lit from the ceiling. Every tier here costs less than the camera body people think they need.
