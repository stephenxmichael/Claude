Before You Record
Lens locked so it cannot switch mid-take
Focus and exposure tapped, held, and nudged down
White balance set by hand, not auto
Mic clipped on and checked through headphones
Overhead lights off, one source to the side
Enough storage for the whole session
Before You Export
A-roll cut holds on its own, with nothing over it
Log converted, then corrected, then graded
Shots match each other across the whole piece
Levels even, music sitting under the voice
Exported at the frame rate you shot
Watched once on a phone before it goes out
