Native Camera
Or Blackmagic
Both live on the same phone and use the same sensor. The difference is how much the app decides for you.

Option 01
Native Camera
The phone makes the calls. You get a very good image with almost no decisions, and you are recording two seconds after you pick it up.
Reach For It When
Something is happening now and you have one chance at it
You are shooting handheld B-roll on the move
The clip is going straight out with light editing
You want Cinematic or Action mode

Option 02
Blackmagic Camera
You make the calls. Shutter, ISO, white balance and codec are all yours, and the app stops quietly changing things between takes.
Reach For It When
You are shooting a talking head you will grade
The light is mixed, tricky, or changing
You need shots to match across a whole shoot
You want Apple Log, or to record to an SSD
The Honest Answer
I use both, most weeks. Native for anything spontaneous, Blackmagic for anything I have set up for. If you are new to this, shoot native until your lighting and framing are consistent. Manual control on top of bad light just gets you a well-exposed bad shot.
