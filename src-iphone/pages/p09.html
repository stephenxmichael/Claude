From Idea
To Published
The same ten steps every time. The order is what keeps a shoot from turning into a salvage job in the edit.
Capture  ·  On The Day
Steps 01–05
01
Idea
One sentence. What is this video actually for.
02
Settings
Preset from page 03. Lock focus, exposure, white balance.
03
A-Roll
Everything you say. Medium first, close on the lines that matter.
04
B-Roll
All six shots, before you break the setup down.
05
Transfer
Off the phone the same day. Dated folder, named properly.
Finish  ·  At The Desk
Steps 06–10
06
Edit
Cut the A-roll first. Nothing else until the words work.
07
Colour
Convert Log, correct, then grade. In that order.
08
Audio
Levels matched, room noise down, music under the voice.
09
Export
H.264, at the frame rate you shot. Do not re-time it.
10
Publish
Caption, cover frame, out. Keep the project file.

Transfer before you sleep. Footage left on a phone is footage you will not use.
Words First
