Lighting And Audio
Nothing else on this list changes an iPhone image as fast, and both are mostly free.
Light
Direction beats brightness
M07.1
PhotoWindow light, 45°16:9 · 213 × 152
Same subject as M07.2
Good
Face the window, then turn about 45 degrees off it. Soft, directional, shows shape.
M07.2
PhotoOverhead ceiling light16:9 · 213 × 152
Same subject as M07.1
Bad
A ceiling light directly above you digs shadows into your eyes. Turn it off.
Where To Put It
One source, off to one side, higher than your eyeline. That is the whole setup.
Sound
Distance beats every microphone
M07.3
PhotoWireless lav clipped for a talking head16:9 · 328 × 236 · close crop
Show mic placement on the shirt
A cheap microphone six inches from your mouth beats an expensive one across the room. Clip it a hand's width below your chin, slightly off centre so your collar does not brush it.
Kill The Room First
Close the windows, turn off the fan and the fridge, and get off the hard floor. Echo cannot be removed later, only hidden.
Record A Safety
Let the phone mic run alongside the lav. It has saved more of my shoots than any other habit.
Listen Back Before You Move
Ten seconds on headphones. Cheaper than reshooting the whole thing tomorrow.
Phone Mic Is Fine
Outdoors with no wind, in a quiet room, within about two feet of your face, or on B-roll where the audio gets replaced anyway. Anywhere else, clip something on.
