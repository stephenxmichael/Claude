Then, and only then, the look
Shutter, Without The Theory
24fps ×2 1/48 The film look. My default.
30fps ×2 1/60 Standard for social.
60fps ×2 1/120 Only when you will slow it down.
Double the frame rate. That is the whole rule. Faster looks choppy, slower turns to mush.
Two Things Log Will Not Forgive
Exposure
Expose slightly brighter than looks right on screen, but never let the highlights clip. Use false colour or zebras. Underexposed Log turns to noise the moment you grade it.
White Balance
Set it by hand and lock it. Auto drifts between takes, and matching shots that each drifted differently is the worst job in the edit.
If you are not going to grade it, do not shoot it. Log straight to the feed looks worse than native.
