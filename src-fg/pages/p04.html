Why These,
And Not The Defaults

24 fps is the frame rate film has used for a century. Your eye reads it as motion picture before you have done anything else.

Log flattens the image on purpose. It looks wrong straight out of the phone, and that is the point. It holds the highlight and shadow information you need when you grade.

Locking white balance and exposure stops the phone re-deciding the image halfway through your shot.
