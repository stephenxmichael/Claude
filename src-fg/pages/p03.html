The Foundation
Get Your
iPhone Right

Before you download another app or build out a rig, get the fundamentals right.

A clean lens, the right frame rate, good light and intentional composition will improve your footage before you touch anything advanced.

Seriously.
Wipe The Lens.
Your phone lives in your hand, your pocket and your bag. Fingerprints and oil soften the image before you have even pressed record.
Start clean.

Choose The Frame RateFor The Shot
4K / 30
Everyday Content
My default for most talking heads, lifestyle content and natural motion.
4K / 24
Cinematic Motion
24 gives you the motion cadence associated with film. I don’t use it just because someone called it cinematic. On an iPhone it can feel choppier depending on the shot and the movement.
4K / 60
Slow It Down
I reach for 60 when I know I want the option to slow the footage down in the edit.
These are my practical defaults, not universal requirements
Use The Lens.
Don’t Just Zoom.
Different lenses change the perspective and depth of the image. Pick the one that frames the shot instead of leaning on digital zoom.

Light First
 
The iPhone produces an incredible image when you give it good light. Look at where it comes from, what it is doing to your subject, and whether you can move first.

Compose Before
You Record

Turn on your grid. Look at subject placement, balance, foreground, background and depth before you hit record.

The settings can be perfect and the shot can still be boring.
