Sound
Sound
Keeps It.

People forgive a soft image. They do not forgive not being able to hear you.

Audio is the cheapest upgrade you can make.

Three Fixes
01
Get the mic closer than feels necessary.
02
Record where there are soft surfaces, not hard ones.
03
Listen back before you shoot the second take.
If they can’t hear it,
they won’t finish it.
