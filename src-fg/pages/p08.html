The Rig

You do not need a rig to start. You need one when the friction of not having it costs more than carrying it.

Everything here earns its place by removing a problem I actually had.

01Phone Mount
A cage or a clamp. Something you can hold without covering the lens.
02Tripod
Small and fast to open. The one you will actually bring.
03Microphone
Wireless or shotgun. Closer beats more expensive, every time.
04One Light
A single small panel. You are shaping the light, not flooding the room.
05Power
A battery pack. Recording for a long time will drain the phone.
06Storage
External drive or an offload habit. ProRes fills a phone fast.
