The
Workflow

The gear question is the one everybody asks. It is almost never the one holding them back.

Six stages. The same six, every time, until you stop having to think about them.

Stage 01
Shoot
Get coverage, not takes. Wide, medium, close, before the camera moves.
Stage 02
Offload
Off the phone the same day. Your camera roll is not an archive.
Stage 03
Select
Watch it once. Mark what works. Do not start cutting yet.
Stage 04
Cut
Structure first. Nothing gets polished before the shape is right.
Stage 05
Color
Correct, then grade. In that order, every single time.
Stage 06
Ship
The version that goes out beats the one you keep improving.
