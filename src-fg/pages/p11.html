Control The Image
01Shutter about double the frame rate.
02Lock white balance when you can.
03Keep ISO as controlled as the scene allows.
04ND to hold your shutter in bright light.
05Log, then correct, then create.
Light & Sound
01Look at the direction before adding a light.
02Turn off overhead light you do not want.
03Add light only when the room is not giving you what you need.
04Get the microphone closer to the source.
05Reduce room noise where you can.
06Monitor your audio when your setup allows.
