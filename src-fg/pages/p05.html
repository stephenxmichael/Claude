The Image

Log gives me room. Color correction gets the image back to neutral. The grade is where I decide how I want it to feel.

Those are three different jobs.

Control The Image Before You Grade ItBefore The Color Pass
Frame Rate
Choose it for how the footage needs to move.
Shutter
A starting point is about double the frame rate. 24→1/48, 30→1/60, 60→1/120.
White Balance
If consistency matters, do not let it shift mid-shot. Lock it when your workflow allows.
ISO
As controlled as the scene allows. More ISO is not free light.
ND
Cuts light in bright conditions so you keep the shutter you already chose.

Capture Information
Log is supposed to look flat. I am not trying to make the image look finished in camera. I am trying to capture one that gives me more room to work in post.
Protect the image now. Make it pretty later.
